    Acceptable use policy: Societal Analytics Lab services

    Draft

    This is a draft, to be reviewed before use. Items marked TODO still need to be filled in or confirmed.

    Version: draft, November 2026

    This acceptable use policy (AUP) applies to everyone who uses a service operated by the Societal Analytics Lab: the lab's AmCAT and AnnoTinder servers, Whisper, Ollama, and the shared OIDC provider (see services covered). By using these services, you agree to this policy.

    It does not apply to AmCAT or AnnoTinder servers hosted by other teams. Those teams set their own policies.

    Purpose

    The services are provided for research and education. Use them only for these purposes, and in line with the rules of your own institution.

    Your account

    • Your account is personal. Don't share it, or your API keys, with others.
    • Protect your password and your second factor (passkey or authenticator app). Keep your recovery codes in a safe place.
    • Report a lost second factor, a leaked API key, or any suspected misuse of your account immediately to TODO: lab contact email, or to your organization's admin for the shared OIDC provider.

    Data

    • Only upload data that you are allowed to use, and handle it according to the agreements that apply to it (e.g. your data management plan, licenses, and consent of research participants).
    • As a project admin, you are responsible for who you give access to your project's data.
    • Servers that use the shared OIDC provider must not hold sensitive research data (see Choosing an OIDC provider).

    Not allowed

    • Using the services for anything illegal, or in violation of the rights of others.
    • Trying to access data, accounts or systems that you have not been given access to.
    • Circumventing or testing security measures without permission from the lab.
    • Disrupting the services, or using them in a way that prevents others from using them (e.g. excessive automated requests).
    • Using the shared GPU services (Whisper, Ollama) for purposes unrelated to research or education. TODO: fair use limits, if any.

    Logging and monitoring

    To keep the services secure, we log logins and actions (see the privacy statement). These logs are used for security, for investigating incidents, and to enforce this policy.

    Consequences

    If this policy is violated, we may suspend or remove accounts, API keys or access to projects, and inform your institution. Serious violations may be reported to the authorities.

    Availability

    The services are provided with best effort, without guarantees about availability or data loss. Keep your own copies of important data.
