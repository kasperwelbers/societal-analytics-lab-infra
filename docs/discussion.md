# About this section

The pages in this section record recent decisions and the information behind them: what was chosen, which alternatives were considered, and the evaluations that led there. They are not set in stone: when circumstances change (new requirements, a provider that changes its behaviour, feedback from VU IT), the decisions can be revisited, and these pages are where that discussion starts.

* [Decisions](./decisions.md): the choices behind the current setup.
* [2FA: where should it live?](./auth-2fa.md): whether the second factor belongs at the login provider or in our software.
* [Provider comparison](./auth-providers.md): the identity providers that were evaluated and tested.
